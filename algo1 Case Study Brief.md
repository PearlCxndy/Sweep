# Technical Product Case Study

---

## 01 — Overview

This case study assesses your ability to research a specific user, translate those insights into a working product, make thoughtful product and technical decisions, and clearly explain your reasoning.

**Please make sure you send across your deliverables 6 hours before your interview slot.**

---

## 02 — The Task

### Build a shopping list app.

Build a shopping list application and present both the product and your codebase to the algo1 team.

### 01 Research

*Understand who you're building for.*

- Define a specific target shopper and explain their needs, behaviours, and challenges.
- Identify problems with existing tools or workflows.
- Use your findings to justify the features you build.

### 02 Build

*Ship something real.*

- Develop a working shopping list application that serves the shopper you have defined.
- Use any language, framework, library, database, or AI tool.

### 03 Product Walkthrough

*Presentation, in-office.*

- Introduce your shopper and research.
- Demonstrate your application.
- Explain your product decisions and trade-offs.

### 04 Code Review

*Deep-dive with engineering, in-office.*

- Walk through the architecture and codebase.
- Explain your technology choices.
- Discuss how AI tools were used.
- Answer questions from the engineering team.
